About Us

Who We Are

Sastralian's Media Unit

ASMU (Ananda Sastralaya Media Unit) is the heartbeat of our school's digital presence. We are a passionate team of students dedicated to documenting school life, creating engaging content, and building a strong online community.

From covering school events and sports days to producing creative reels and broadcasts, we ensure every memorable moment is captured and shared with the world. Our mission is to showcase the vibrant spirit of Ananda Sastralaya.

Our Mission

To capture and share every school moment
